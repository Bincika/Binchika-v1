Applications to become a Binchika verifier are not open yet. They open only once our privacy notice is in force, and we will announce the date on binchika.com/verifiers and on @getbinchika.

The published plan, with proposed dates that move with the privacy notice:
• Applications: a two-week window, proposed for 2–16 November 2026. Late applications are never reviewed.
• Screening: proposed for 17 November – 4 December, with the same published questions for everyone.
• First selections: early December. Each selected applicant receives a seat code by email, only from noreply@binchika.com, and has 24 hours to claim it.
• Training: a first block in mid-December, a holiday pause from 20 December to 3 January, then a second block and a full dry run from 4 to 10 January.

If the privacy notice is late, the application window and screening shrink; the holiday pause and the election days (16 January and 6 February 2027) do not move.
