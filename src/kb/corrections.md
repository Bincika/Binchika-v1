No single person can change a result. A correction needs three separate seats: the verifier who flags it, a different Regional Verifier who escalates it, and the BinchikAdmin, who records it after the AI re-check.

A correction becomes a new public ledger entry, so nothing is erased. Planned next: a quorum of at least 5 of 7 randomly assigned Regional Verifiers. The rules are on binchika.com/governance.

To report a figure you believe is wrong, use the contact form and choose "Report an error in our data", so a person can look at it.
