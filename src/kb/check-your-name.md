Before you can vote, your name has to be on INEC's register, and you check it on INEC's own portal.

• Go to display.inecnigeria.org. This is INEC's official site; Binchika is not part of it.
• Create an account and sign in, as the portal asks, then choose your state → local government area → ward → polling unit, or type your polling-unit code if you know it.
• If something is wrong or missing, report it to INEC through the claims-and-objections process, at your INEC local government (registration area) office or through INEC's official channels. Binchika does not take, process or forward claims or objections; only INEC does.

The final register is due to be certified and published on 15 December 2026. Step-by-step help is on binchika.com/vote.
