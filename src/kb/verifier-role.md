Verifiers look at the citizen photos coming in from their own state and flag anything that doesn't add up. They never change a result.

• It is unpaid volunteer work. Expecting payment rules an applicant out, which protects verifiers from anyone trying to buy influence.
• You choose your state of origin or of residence when you apply, and it can't be changed later.
• Publicly, every verifier is known only by a sequential 5-character ID, for example V0042.
• A correction needs three separate seats: the verifier who flags it, a different Regional Verifier and the BinchikAdmin, after AI re-investigation.

Applications are not open yet. The roles, the rules and the timetable are on binchika.com/verifiers.
