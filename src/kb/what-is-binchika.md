Binchika is Africa's First Decentralised Election Ledger: a citizen transparency layer, not an election system. It counts what citizens photograph, compares it with what INEC uploads, and chains every step into a ledger anyone can verify.

Binchika is not an election authority, and it is not a replacement for INEC or the courts. Only INEC declares official results.

It is open source: a forkable protocol any African civic group can run. More is on binchika.com/about.
