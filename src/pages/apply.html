Our ethics and data standard

This standard applies wherever the Binchika Protocol runs. For Nigeria it implements the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Other countries swap in their own law (for example Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012, South Africa's POPIA, or Senegal's Law 2008-12), guided by the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention).

  • Consent, clearly asked. You apply by choice, and can withdraw at any time before or after selection.
  • Only what is needed. Your full name, state of origin or residence, an email, and your voter card number stored only as a salted hash plus its last four characters (never the full number). We never ask for your NIN or BVN. Your name is private: it is used only to check the name you type when you sign the verifier pledge, and only the BinchikAdmin can see it.
  • One purpose. Data is used only to screen, select and run the verifier network. It is never used for marketing and never shared with parties.
  • Short retention. Applications that are not selected are deleted 90 days after the final election in the cycle.
  • Your rights. You can see, correct or delete your data. Use the contact page.
  • Assessment first. A data-protection impact assessment is completed and the privacy notice is reviewed before applications open.
  • Fair selection. The same published questions, an automated bias check, a regional spread check, and no favours. Applicants are addressed only by their assigned number after selection.

This is our standard, not legal advice. The privacy notice will be reviewed by a qualified data-protection professional before applications open.

Proposed timetable (Nigeria 2027)

This timetable avoids the Christmas and New Year break and keeps a buffer before both election days. It is a proposal, and the final dates will be announced on this page.

  1. 2–16 Nov 2026 · Applications open (2 weeks). Only after the privacy notice is published.
  2. 17 Nov–4 Dec · Screening. Published questions, bias check and regional spread check.
  3. 7 Dec · First wave announced. Seat codes go out by email only. Selectees have 24 hours to claim their seat at binchika.com/join (email + password), then confirm their email and sign the verifier pledge.
  4. 8–12 Dec · Backfill. Places not taken pass to the next applicant from the same state, who has 6 hours.
  5. 14–19 Dec · Training block 1 (online, evenings): duties, privacy, and how to flag.
  6. 20 Dec–3 Jan · Holiday pause. No deadlines.
  7. 4–10 Jan 2027 · Training block 2 and a full dry run on test data.
  8. 16 Jan · Presidential & National Assembly. Then 72 hours of active verifying.
  9. 6 Feb · Governorship & State Assembly. Then 72 hours of active verifying.

Which state would you verify for?

You can only ever verify this one state (or its region, for regional roles).

1. Do you expect payment or any other compensation for verifying?
2. Are you currently a member, official, candidate, polling agent or employee of any political party or campaign?
3. In the last four years, have you held party office or stood as a candidate?
4. Will you work for the electoral body (INEC), or as an election official, in these elections?
5. Are you 18 or older, with a valid voter card?
6. Can you be available on both election days and for the 72 hours after each?
7. Would you flag a mismatch even if it hurt a party you like?
8. Will you be deployed as an accredited election observer in these elections?
