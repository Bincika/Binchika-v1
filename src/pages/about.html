About

A citizen transparency layer that does the maths in public.

Binchika is open-source software for comparing citizens' reports of posted result sheets with official uploads. Only {{BODY}} declares results.

What it is not

Binchika is not an election management body, and it does not declare results. A tally here is not a result, a projection or a winner.

Run it for your own country

{{COUNTRY}} is a fictional country, included so that a fresh copy runs. To use Binchika for a real election, replace the example tenant with your own, every value with a source, and rewrite these pages for your country. The guide is docs/TENANT-CONFIG.md in the code.

You are responsible for following your own country's election and data-protection law.
