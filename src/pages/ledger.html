Entry

From photo to public entry

1 · Photo

A citizen photographs or reports the posted result sheet. Their handle becomes a one-way hash.

2 · Read

Machine reading extracts each party's votes; uncertain reads go to people.

3 · Agree

At least 85% of at least 3 independent reports must match for high confidence.

4 · Compare

The tally is checked against INEC's IReV upload and the pre-election register.

5 · Chain

A new entry is hashed onto the chain. Nothing can be edited, only added.

High confidence

At least 85% of independent reports agree on every party's count, from at least 3 reporters. Only these reach the ledger as tallies.

Mismatch with IReV

The consensus citizen tally differs from INEC's IReV upload. The flag shows each party's difference. It is not an accusation.

Correction

A verifier raised a flag, a different Regional Verifier escalated it, AI re-investigated it and the BinchikAdmin recorded the correction. Corrections are new entries, so history is never edited.
