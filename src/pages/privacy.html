Privacy notice · template

This is a template, not a notice.

This example deployment collects nothing through applications or reports. Before you collect personal data, write your own notice under your own country's law, with advice from someone qualified. Only {{BODY}} declares election results.

Not in force. Applications and reports stay closed unless APPLICATIONS_OPEN and PRIVACY_NOTICE_VERSION are both set. Set them only when your own notice is in force.

1. Who is responsible

State the data controller's legal name, a business location and a contact route.

2. What you collect, and why

List each group of people, the data, the purpose and the lawful basis. Include your contact form and any request forms.

3. Service providers, and data outside the country

Name each provider, where it stores data, and your legal basis for any transfer.

4. Photos of result sheets

The design default is that photos are never published. A photo can show people, names and signatures, and those people have not agreed to be published.

5. How long you keep it

Give a period for each kind of data, and make sure something actually deletes it.

6. People's rights

Explain how to see, correct or delete data, how to complain to the regulator, and how fast you answer.
