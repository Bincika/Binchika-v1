In short

  • Minimum data. We never ask for your BVN, NIN, bank details, biometrics, passwords for other services, or a photo of your ID. A voter card number is kept only as a one-way, salted fingerprint plus its last four characters.
  • One purpose each. Data collected to screen a verifier is used only for that, and to match the name typed when signing the pledge. It is never used for marketing, sold, or shared with any party, campaign or candidate.
  • Pseudonymous in public. We show a verifier's seat and public ID (for example SZV-073 · V0042), never their name. Only the BinchikAdmin keeps the separate record that links a public ID to a person, and we keep access to it to a minimum.
  • Ineligible answers are never stored. If the published screening rules rule you out, you are told why on screen and nothing is saved.
  • You have rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

3. Answers that touch on political affiliation

Two screening questions ask whether you hold, or recently held, a party or campaign role. Under the NDPA, data revealing political opinions is sensitive personal data. We ask only because verifiers must be independent. We store the answers only for applicants who pass (whose answer is “no”), and only with your explicit consent, given by ticking the consent box before you submit. If your answer rules you out, it is never stored.

4. Automated screening, and a human review

Eligibility is checked by eight published rules (the same code runs in your browser and on our server). Every eligible applicant gets the same score, and ties go to whoever applied first. Choosing “not sure” on the impartiality question sends your application to a person for review. If you think a rule was applied wrongly, ask for a human review through the contact form. No other profiling or automated decision is made about you.

5. Who can see your data

  • The public sees seat numbers, public IDs, whether a seat is taken, the public seat history (seat, public ID, pledge version and time, never names or reasons), flags and their outcomes (by public ID), and the evidence ledger.
  • Other verifiers see only seat numbers and public IDs.
  • OpsAdmins (three seats at most) can see verifier records by public ID to help with reviews. They cannot see applicants' names, emails or answers, read pledge names, or link a public ID to a person.
  • The BinchikAdmin (one person, whose sign-in needs an authenticator app) can link a public ID to a person and read pledge names, through server functions that check every request. When choosing whom to invite, the BinchikAdmin sees state, role and application date, not names or emails. The BinchikAdmin is also the only person who can open emails kept in the dashboard (section 2).
  • Nobody else. We never sell, rent or share personal data with parties, campaigns, candidates, advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose data to authorities only where Nigerian law requires it, and we would tell you unless the law forbids it.

7. Photos of result sheets

Result sheets are public documents, but a photo can catch people, faces, names, signatures or location data. We never publish photos. When a photo or video arrives, we remove camera and location data (EXIF) and record its fingerprint, then keep it in private storage for verification only. Only the verifiers for that area can view it, after signing in with an authenticator app. This will not change unless people and identifying marks (faces, names, signatures, codes and captions) can be reliably redacted, and this notice is updated first. owner decision 29 Oct 2026 · for review

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can:

  • ask what we hold about you, and for a copy;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict or object to its use;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not undo processing already done);
  • ask for a human to review the automated screening.

Use the contact form and choose “Data request”. We will check it is really you, by replying to the email address on record, and answer within one month owner decision 29 Oct 2026 · for review. We assess requests to correct, delete, restrict or object under the NDPA, and erase personal data without undue delay when we no longer need it and have no other lawful reason to keep it. We do not assume that a public ID or a fingerprint is anonymous. Public tallies are designed to contain no personal data; where a ledger entry has to change, we add a correction rather than rewrite history.

You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at any time. We would rather you tell us first, so we can put it right.

10. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections everywhere.
  • Database rules that let each person read only what their role allows.
  • One-way fingerprints for invite codes, voter card numbers, messaging handles and IP addresses.
  • An authenticator app required for every admin.
  • Emails kept for the dashboard can be opened only by the BinchikAdmin, and our mail logs record no addresses or message content.
  • Admin powers checked again on the server for every request.
  • No secrets in our public code.

Our code is open source, so anyone can check these claims.

If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the NDPC and you as the NDPA requires.

11. Age

Verifiers must be 18 or older and hold a voter card. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.
