How it works

  1. Report. A citizen sends a photo or report of the posted result sheet.
  2. Compare. Independent reports for a polling station are compared with {{BODY}}'s {{PORTAL}} upload. A mismatch is flagged with its sources and reason.
  3. Publish. A station is published only when at least 3 independent reports agree on at least 85% of the figures and a trained volunteer confirms them.
  4. Examine. Every published tally goes into a public, append-only, hash-chained ledger that anyone can re-verify.
